CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("Cell Cave," "we," "us," or "our") provides the All Video Downloader & Saver mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects or processes, why it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Summary of Our Privacy Practices

  • The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location to use the App.
  • Video links that you paste into the App are processed locally on your device and are not sent to Cell Cave servers.
  • Videos and other media you download are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to Cell Cave servers.
  • Media placed in Gallery Vault and the Vault PIN or password are stored and verified locally on your device and are not sent to Cell Cave servers.
  • The App may request access to photos, videos, music, and audio only to provide the media features you choose to use.
  • Third-party services used for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, push notifications, remote configuration, consent management, machine-learning features, and billing may process device, usage, diagnostic, advertising, consent, and purchase-related information under their own privacy policies.
  • The free version of the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • You may request deletion of any information held by Cell Cave at any time. See Section 14.

2. What the App Does

The App is a link-based video downloader.

You copy a link to content you have permission to save, paste it into the App, and the App retrieves and saves the supported media to your device.

The App also provides a download manager, offline playback, media-management features, and Gallery Vault controls.

The App is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any social media platform or content provider.

3. Information You Provide

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, your message, screenshots, and any technical details you include.

We use this only to respond to you, investigate problems, protect the App, and improve our services.

Please do not send passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information in a support request.

4. Information Processed on Your Device

Pasted Video Links

When you paste or enter a supported video link, the App processes that link locally on your device to identify and retrieve the requested content.

Cell Cave does not receive or store the pasted link through the App's normal functionality.

The website or platform hosting the linked video will independently receive your request and may process information such as your IP address, device and browser details, cookies, and activity under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cell Cave does not control and is not responsible for that processing.

Downloaded Videos and Media

Downloaded videos and related files are saved locally on your device, or to a location you select.

Cell Cave does not upload these files to its servers and cannot view them.

You can delete downloaded files from within the App, or using your device's file manager, Gallery, or system settings.

Download History and Settings

Your download list and App preferences are stored locally on your device.

Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App may remove them, subject to your device settings and any applicable device-level backup behaviour.

Gallery Vault

Media placed in Gallery Vault remains locally stored on your device.

Your Vault PIN or password is also stored and verified locally and is not sent to Cell Cave servers.

Because Cell Cave does not receive the PIN, we cannot recover it or restore access if you forget it, uninstall the App, clear App data, lose your device, or remove the relevant local files.

5. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, depending on your Android version and the features you use.

Permissions are requested only when a feature requires them, and no permission is used to upload your personal files to Cell Cave.

Photos and Videos

Android permissions such as READ_MEDIA_VIDEO and READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or legacy storage permissions on older Android versions, may be used to save, select, display, play, organise, share, delete, or protect videos and images.

On supported Android versions, you may grant full access, limited access to selected media, or no access.

If you deny access, features that require access to the relevant media may not work.

Music and Audio

The App may request Music and Audio access, including READ_MEDIA_AUDIO or legacy storage access on older Android versions.

This access is used to save, select, play, organise, share, move, export, or protect audio files when you choose to use an audio-related feature.

Notifications

POST_NOTIFICATIONS on supported Android versions may be used to show locally generated download progress and completion alerts and to receive service messages or other push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Internet and Network State

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used to retrieve the video you requested, check connectivity, display advertisements, validate purchases, record consent choices, and communicate with the third-party services described in Section 7.

Foreground Service

Where required by the Android version and the App's active download implementation, a foreground-service permission may be used to keep a user-requested download running and display a persistent notification while the task is active.

Advertising ID

Where available, the Google advertising identifier may be processed by Google AdMob and related Google services to deliver, limit, personalise, and measure advertising, subject to applicable law and your consent choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID through your device's Google settings where that option is available.

You can deny or revoke any permission through Android settings, although features that depend on it will stop working.
