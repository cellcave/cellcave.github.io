1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED (“CELL CAVE,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Android application Status Downloader: Video Saver (the “App”).

The App allows users to preview, save, view, manage, share, repost and delete eligible photo and video status content that is available on their device.

The App does not require a CELL CAVE account or login. We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number, postal address, password or precise GPS location to use the App’s main status-saving features.

3. Status Photos, Videos and Local Processing

The App may require access to eligible status photos and videos stored on your device.

This access is used to display available media and allow you to save or manage a local copy.

During the App’s normal status-saving functionality, status photos and videos are processed locally on the device and are not uploaded to a CELL CAVE server.

The App may locally use file names, paths, sizes, dates, thumbnails, cached items or recent-item references as needed to display and manage media.

These items are used for App functionality and remain subject to your device’s storage and permission controls.

When you save a status, the App creates a copy in an available folder on your device.

The saved copy remains until you delete it.

When you choose to share or repost media, you direct the App to send that file to another application or recipient.

The receiving service’s own privacy policy and terms then apply.

Deleting media through the App removes the selected local copy where Android permits.

Depending on your device, deleted content may temporarily remain in a system trash or recycle bin.

Copies already shared elsewhere are not affected.

4. Your Responsibility for Saved Content

Status media may belong to another person and may contain their personal information.

You should only save, share or repost content that you own or have permission to use.

Do not use the App to infringe copyright, violate another person’s privacy, harass someone, break applicable law or violate the terms of the service from which the content originated.

CELL CAVE does not receive or control locally saved status media and cannot remotely delete files from a user’s device.
