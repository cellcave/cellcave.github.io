Privacy Policy for Document Reader: Read All PDF

This Privacy Policy explains how Cell Cave handles information when you use Document Reader: Read All PDF, including document and file access, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, payments, and third-party services.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Contents

1. Overview 2. Information 3. File Access 4. Analytics 5. Advertising 6. Subscription 7. Third Parties 8. Data Retention 9. Your Choices 10. Children 11. Security 12. International Processing 13. Changes 14. Contact

1. Overview

Document Reader: Read All PDF is provided by Cell Cave.

The app is designed to help users open and read supported documents and files on their device.

The app does not require users to create a Cell Cave account or provide a name, email address, phone number, or password to use its main functionality.

2. Information We Handle

Information we do not directly request

The app does not require you to provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • A Cell Cave account or password
  • Precise GPS location
  • Credit or debit card details directly to Cell Cave

Technical and usage information

The app uses third-party analytics and advertising technologies. Depending on those services, device settings, region, and applicable privacy choices, third-party providers may process technical information such as:

  • Device and app information
  • Operating system information
  • App interactions and usage events
  • Advertising or app identifiers
  • IP address and network information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic or performance information
  • Approximate location inferred from technical information such as an IP address
Precise Location

Document Reader: Read All PDF does not request precise GPS location for its core document reading functionality.

Contacting support

If you contact Cell Cave by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any other information you voluntarily include so that we can respond to your request.

3. Files, Documents and Storage Access

Document Reader: Read All PDF may request permission to access files, documents, media, or device storage when this access is required to open and read supported files selected by the user.

Documents and files used through the app's document-reading functionality are handled locally on the user's device.

Cell Cave does not upload your documents or files to a Cell Cave server as part of the app's normal document-reading functionality.

You can manage or revoke applicable file and storage permissions through your Android device settings. Some app functionality may not work correctly when required permissions are disabled.

4. Firebase Analytics

Document Reader: Read All PDF uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google.

Firebase Analytics helps measure app usage and understand how users interact with the app.

Firebase Analytics is active for both users of the free version and paid subscribers.

Depending on Google's service configuration, Firebase may process usage events, device or app information, identifiers, and related technical analytics information.

5. Advertising

Document Reader: Read All PDF offers both a free version and a paid subscription.

Free users

Users of the free version may see advertisements.

Advertising services used by the app may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Mintegral
  • Pangle
  • Liftoff

Advertising providers may process device, advertising, network, interaction, and related technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable privacy choices.

Paid subscribers

Paid subscribers receive an ad-free experience and advertisements are not shown through the paid subscription experience.

Firebase Analytics remains active for both free and paid users as described in this policy.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

Document Reader: Read All PDF offers a paid subscription that provides an ad-free experience.

Subscription purchases and payment processing are handled through Google Play.

Cell Cave does not directly collect or process your full credit or debit card number for these Google Play subscription transactions.

Google may process billing, payment, account, transaction, and related information according to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Subscription management, renewal, and cancellation are handled through your Google Play account.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Documents and files opened through the app are handled locally on the user's device and are not stored on Cell Cave servers as part of the app's normal document-reading functionality.

Users control files stored on their own device using Android storage and file management tools.

Analytics, advertising, billing, and other information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to each provider's own retention practices.

If you contact us for support, Cell Cave may retain the communication for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request or maintain appropriate support records.

9. Your Privacy Choices

  • You can manage file, document, media, and storage permissions through Android settings.
  • You can manage relevant advertising and privacy options through your device and applicable consent controls.
  • You may use the paid subscription for an ad-free app experience.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.
  • You can contact Cell Cave regarding applicable privacy questions or requests.

Available privacy rights and choices may vary depending on your country or region.

10. Children's Privacy

Document Reader: Read All PDF is not directed to children under the age of 13.

Cell Cave does not knowingly require children under 13 to create an account or directly provide personal contact information through this app.

If a parent or guardian believes a child under 13 has directly provided personal information to Cell Cave, please contact us so we can review the request.

11. Data Security

Documents opened through the app's normal document-reading functionality are handled locally on the user's device instead of being uploaded to Cell Cave servers.

Information processed by Firebase, advertising networks, Google Play, or other third-party providers is subject to the security practices of those providers.

No device, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International Data Processing

Third-party providers integrated with the app may operate in multiple countries.

As a result, analytics, advertising, payment, or related technical information may be processed outside the country in which you live.

Such processing is subject to the applicable provider's policies and relevant legal requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Cell Cave may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, third-party services, privacy practices, or applicable requirements change.

When the policy changes, the "Last updated" date displayed at the top of this page will be revised.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
