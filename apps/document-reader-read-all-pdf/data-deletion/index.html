Data Deletion

How to delete app data from your Android device or request deletion of information held by CELL CAVE.

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Delete Local Files and App Data

Delete individual documents or scans through the App or Android's file manager. To remove local app settings and stored app data, open Android Settings > Apps > All Document Reader > Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names may vary by device. You can also uninstall the App.

Documents and scans saved in shared folders may remain after you clear app data or uninstall. Delete those files separately if you want them removed. Keep a copy of any files you want to retain.

2. Request Deletion by Email

To request deletion of support correspondence or other personal information held directly by CELL CAVE, email us with the subject Data Deletion Request.

Send your deletion request tocellcave70@gmail.com

Mention All Document Reader: Sign PDF and describe the information you would like deleted. We may need to verify details reasonably necessary to process your request.

3. What Can Be Deleted

All Document Reader: Sign PDF does not use a CELL CAVE account. Your documents and scans saved on your device are managed through the App or Android's file manager. Clearing app storage removes app-managed local data such as settings, cache, and recent-file records, subject to Android behavior.

You can ask CELL CAVE to delete eligible support emails or other personal information it holds directly.

4. What May Be Retained

CELL CAVE may retain information as reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or prevent abuse. Google Play and other third-party providers manage information they control under their own privacy and retention policies.

Manage or cancel a Google Play subscription separately in your Google Play account. Clearing storage or uninstalling the App does not cancel it.

5. Privacy Information

Read the App's Privacy Policy for details about document storage, third-party services, retention, and your privacy rights.
