This Privacy Policy explains how CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Find My Phone By Clapping (“App”).

The App provides features such as clap detection, voice/passcode detection, motion or touch alerts, pocket detection, charger-disconnection alerts, sound alerts, flashlight alerts, vibration alerts, and related phone-finding functionality.

The App does not require registration or creation of a user account. Some information is processed locally on your device, while third-party services used for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, app configuration, and purchases may process technical information as explained below.

1. Information Processed on Your Device

Several App features process information directly on your device.

Microphone, Clap and Voice Detection

When clap detection is enabled, the App uses your microphone to analyze sounds in real time and detect a clap. Clap detection is intended to work locally and we do not operate a server for storing your clap recordings.

If you enable an optional voice or spoken-passcode feature, the App may use Android or your device provider's speech-recognition service to convert speech into text. Depending on the service, audio may be processed by that provider, including through its servers. Such processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

For Google speech services, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Custom Sounds and Audio

The App may allow you to record a custom alert sound or select an audio file from your device. Such files may be stored in the App's local/private storage for use as an alert.

We do not intentionally upload these personal audio files to our own servers.

Sensors and Device Events

Features such as anti-touch, motion detection, pocket detection, and charger alerts may use information from:

  • Motion or accelerometer sensors;
  • Proximity sensors;
  • Screen state;
  • Charger connection status; and
  • Related device events.

This information is primarily processed in real time to provide the feature you activate.

Settings and Security

The App may locally store preferences such as alert sounds, vibration or flashlight settings, sensitivity, language, theme, volume, Premium status, and feature settings.

If a PIN, recovery answer, or similar security option is provided, relevant security values may be stored locally using protected or cryptographic methods.

Local App information normally remains on your device until you remove it, clear App data, or uninstall the App.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Firebase and Google Services

The App uses several Google Firebase and Google Play technologies.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how users interact with the App. It may process information such as App instance identifiers, Firebase identifiers, device details, App version, sessions, screen or feature interactions, advertising events, purchase-related events, and approximate geographic information.

Firebase Crashlytics and Sessions

Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Sessions help identify crashes, bugs, and stability problems.

Information may include:

  • Crash logs and stack traces;
  • Device model;
  • Operating-system and App version;
  • Session information;
  • Device state during a crash; and
  • Technical identifiers.

Firebase Cloud Messaging and Installations

Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations may process Firebase Installation IDs, notification tokens, and technical App/device information to provide notifications and related functionality.

Cloud Firestore

Cloud Firestore may be used to obtain App configuration, advertising settings, feature information, or similar App-related configuration.

Technical information such as IP addresses and App identifiers may be processed when the App communicates with Firestore.

The App is not intended to upload your private clap recordings or selected personal audio files to Firestore.

6. Permissions and Device Access

Depending on the Android version and feature used, the App may request or declare permissions including:

Microphone

Used for clap detection, voice/passcode functionality, and recording custom sounds.

Camera and Flashlight

Used to control your device's torch or flashlight for alerts. The flashlight feature is not intended to take photographs or record videos.

Notifications

Used for alerts, foreground-service notifications, reminders, and push notifications.

Display Over Other Apps

May be used to show an alert interface when a finder or protection feature is triggered.

Foreground Services, Wake Lock and Boot Access

Used to help enabled detection modes continue running reliably and handle relevant App/service status after device restart.

Vibration and Audio Settings

Used to vibrate your device and control sound or alert functionality.

Audio and Storage Permissions

The App may use READ_MEDIA_AUDIO or legacy Android storage permissions where necessary to allow you to select or use custom audio files.

On supported Android versions, system file pickers may instead provide access only to the file you choose.

Internet and Network Access

Used for advertising, Firebase, purchases, notifications, configuration, analytics, and other online services.

Advertising Permissions

The App may use Google Advertising ID and Android Ad Services/Privacy Sandbox functionality for advertising, attribution, measurement, and related purposes.

Phone State

The APK contains the Android READ_PHONE_STATE permission. This may be used for device or SDK compatibility. We do not intentionally use it to collect your contacts, SMS messages, call history, or telephone conversations.

You can revoke permissions through Android settings, although disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working correctly.
