Switch Smart

Switch Smart is a device-to-device transfer utility designed to help users move supported content from one Android device to another through supported local connection methods.

Google Play — Coming Soon Privacy Policy
App Features

Move supported content between your devices

Switch Smart focuses on making supported Android-to-Android transfers straightforward through local device connections.

01

Phone-to-Phone Transfer

Transfer supported content from an old Android phone to a new Android phone.

02

Local Connection

Connect supported devices using available local Wi-Fi or hotspot-based transfer methods.

03

Multiple Data Types

Move supported contacts, photos, videos, music, documents, files, and other available content.

04

Device Discovery

Use supported connection and discovery methods to help nearby devices establish a transfer session.

05

QR Connection

Where available, QR-based connection can help users connect the sending and receiving devices.

06

Direct Transfer

Supported transfer content is intended to move directly between connected devices rather than being stored by Cell Cave as part of the transfer process.

Supported Content

Available transfer categories may depend on the device, Android version, granted permissions, and the current version of Switch Smart.

  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Music
  • Documents
  • Files
  • Media
  • Supported Apps
