1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Switch Smart Android application ("Switch Smart" or the "App"), provided by Cell Cave ("Cell Cave," "we," "us," or "our"), accesses, uses, transfers, and stores information.

Switch Smart helps users transfer selected content from one Android device to another using the same Wi-Fi network or a hotspot.

The App does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information and Content Accessed by the App

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, Switch Smart may access:

  • Contacts
  • Photos and videos
  • Music and other audio files
  • Documents and other files
  • App installation files selected and made available by the user

The App accesses this content only to display eligible items, let the user choose what to transfer, send selected content, and save received content on the receiving device.

Personal content selected for transfer is processed locally and transferred directly between the user's devices.

It is not sent to, uploaded to, routed through, or stored on Cell Cave servers.

Cell Cave does not use transferred content for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

3. Permissions

Switch Smart requests permissions only when needed for an App feature. These may include:

Photos, Videos, and Media

Used to display and transfer photos, videos, music, and other supported media selected by the user, and to save received media on the receiving device.

Files and Storage

Switch Smart uses Android's system file-selection tools, including the Storage Access Framework, to let users choose files, documents, folders, or supported app installation files for transfer and select where received files are saved.

The App does not require unrestricted access to all files for this purpose.

Contacts

Used to read contacts only when the user chooses to transfer them.

Contacts are transferred directly between the selected devices and are not uploaded to Cell Cave servers.

Camera

Used only to scan a QR code for device connection and transfer setup.

Camera images used for scanning are processed on the device and are not uploaded to Cell Cave servers.

Location

Android may require Location permission to support Wi-Fi or hotspot-based device discovery and connection.

Switch Smart uses this permission only for that functionality. Cell Cave does not use it to track the user's physical location or store location data on its servers.

Network Access

Used to check network availability, establish a local connection between devices, complete Wi-Fi or hotspot transfers, and allow the third-party services described in Section 8 to operate.

Notifications

Used to display transfer progress, status, completion notices, and related service notifications.

Users can disable notifications in Android settings.

Granting permission does not cause transferred content to be uploaded to Cell Cave servers. If a user denies or revokes a permission, the related feature may not work.

4. How Device-to-Device Transfer Works

The user selects one device to send data and another to receive it.

The devices connect using:

  • The same Wi-Fi network; or
  • A hotspot created or joined for the transfer.

A QR code may be scanned to help connect the devices. The user then selects the supported content to transfer.

Transfers occur directly between the connected devices. Cell Cave does not operate an intermediary transfer server and cannot access the transferred content.

Data transferred through both supported methods—same-network Wi-Fi and hotspot—is encrypted during transfer.

Users should connect only to devices they recognize and use trusted Wi-Fi networks or password-protected hotspots.

5. Local Processing and Local Data

Switch Smart handles transferred content locally on the user's devices.

Received content is saved on the receiving device in the location used by the relevant Android system or selected by the user.

The App may also store transfer-related information locally to support its operation and display transfer status or history.

Cell Cave does not receive this local information. The precise local fields may depend on the App version and transfer performed.

6. Storage and Retention

  • Transferred content saved on the receiving device is not automatically deleted. It remains until the user deletes it using the relevant Gallery, Contacts, Music, or Files app.
  • Transfer-related information stored by Switch Smart is not automatically deleted, and the App does not currently provide a separate control for manually deleting transfer history.
  • Users can remove the App's locally stored data by clearing Switch Smart's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Content saved outside the App's private storage may remain after uninstall and must be deleted separately.
  • Third-party services retain information according to their applicable policies and settings.

7. How Information Is Used

Switch Smart uses information and device access to:

  • Discover and connect the user's devices
  • Display transferable content selected or made available by the user
  • Transfer content directly between connected devices
  • Save received content on the receiving device
  • Display transfer progress and status
  • Provide and verify paid features
  • Display and measure advertising
  • Analyze App usage and performance
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems
  • Respond to support or privacy inquiries
  • Meet applicable legal and security obligations

Transferred personal content is not used for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or performance monitoring.

8. Third-Party Services

Switch Smart includes third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and purchases.

These services may process technical information separately from personal content transferred between devices.

Depending on the provider, device settings, region, consent choices, and SDK configuration, technical information may include IP address, approximate location inferred from an IP address, device and App information, advertising or app-instance identifiers, App interactions, ad interactions, diagnostic data, and purchase or subscription status.

Switch Smart does not intentionally provide transferred contacts, photos, videos, music, documents, files, or app installation files to these providers.

8.1 Advertising

Switch Smart displays ads through Google AdMob.

Through AdMob mediation, ads may also be provided by:

  • Meta Audience Network
  • Mintegral
  • Pangle
  • Liftoff Monetize

Advertising providers may process technical information to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, provide personalized advertising.

Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Apps That Use Its Services
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Mintegral Privacy Policy
  • Pangle End User Privacy Policy
  • Liftoff Privacy Policy

Users can manage available advertising controls, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where supported, through Android or Google settings.

8.2 Firebase Services

Switch Smart uses these Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics: processes App interaction and usage information to help us understand how the App is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: processes crash reports, diagnostic information, and relevant device or App information to help identify and fix errors.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: processes performance and diagnostic information, such as App startup or network-performance measurements, to help identify performance problems.

Switch Smart does not intentionally include transferred content, contact names, file names, file paths, or transferred-file contents in Firebase Analytics events, Crashlytics reports, or Performance Monitoring data.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy .

8.3 Google Play Billing

Switch Smart offers paid plans, subscriptions, or in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

Google Play processes payment details; Cell Cave does not receive the user's full payment-card or bank-account details.

The App receives purchase and entitlement information from Google Play, such as whether a purchase or subscription is active, to unlock paid features or remove ads.

Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Google Play account.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service .

9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Cell Cave does not sell, rent, upload, or disclose the user's transferred personal content to advertising, analytics, or other third-party providers.

Information may be disclosed only in these circumstances:

  • To the device selected by the user: selected content is transferred to the receiving device as requested.
  • To third-party services: providers identified in Section 8 may process technical, advertising, analytics, diagnostic, performance, or purchase-related information through their SDKs according to their policies and the App's configuration.
  • For legal or security reasons: Cell Cave may disclose information in its possession when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, or protect users, Cell Cave, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.
  • For support: information voluntarily included in an email to Cell Cave is used to respond to the request.

10. Security

Switch Smart encrypts data transferred directly between devices over both same-network Wi-Fi and hotspot connections.

Transferred content is not routed through Cell Cave servers.

Users remain responsible for selecting the correct receiving device, protecting their devices and hotspot credentials, and deleting transferred content when no longer needed.

No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. User Choices and Permission Management

Users can:

  • Choose which content to transfer
  • Accept or reject a device connection
  • Grant, limit, or revoke permissions through Android settings
  • Turn notifications on or off through Android settings
  • Manage available advertising preferences through Android or Google settings
  • Manage or cancel paid subscriptions through Google Play
  • Delete received content through the relevant Gallery, Contacts, Music, or Files app
  • Clear Switch Smart's local App data or uninstall the App

12. Data Deletion and Privacy Rights

Switch Smart has no account system and does not store transferred content on Cell Cave servers.

Cell Cave therefore cannot remotely access or delete content stored on users' devices.

Users control that content and can delete it through their device applications and settings.

Users may clear Switch Smart's locally stored App data through Android settings or uninstall the App.

Content saved outside the App's private storage may remain until deleted separately.

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning personal information processed by Cell Cave, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection.

Contact us at cellcave70@gmail.com .

Because the App has no accounts, we may not be able to identify technical information processed under pseudonymous identifiers without additional information.

Third-party providers may offer their own privacy-request mechanisms under the policies linked in Section 8.

13. Children's Privacy

Switch Smart is a general-audience utility intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

The App does not ask users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number.

Advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, and performance-monitoring services may process technical information as described in this Policy.

If the App's target audience or treatment of younger users changes, Cell Cave will update the App's configuration, disclosures, and this Privacy Policy as required.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information directly to Cell Cave, they may contact us at cellcave70@gmail.com .

14. International Processing

Personal content transferred through Switch Smart remains between the user's connected devices and is not transferred internationally by Cell Cave.

Third-party providers identified in Section 8 may process technical information in other countries, subject to their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

15. Third-Party Ads and Links

Ads or links displayed in Switch Smart may open websites or applications operated by third parties.

Their content and privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Cell Cave may update this Privacy Policy when Switch Smart's features, permissions, third-party services, or data practices change.

The revised policy will show an updated date.

Where appropriate, we may also provide notice in the App or through the App's Google Play listing.
