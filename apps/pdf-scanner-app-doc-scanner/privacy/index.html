1. Introduction

CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("Cell Cave", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PDF Scanner App – Doc Scanner Android application ("App").

The App is designed primarily to process documents directly on your device. Your scans, imported images, PDFs, recognised text, signatures and other document content are not intentionally uploaded to Cell Cave servers.

We do not operate document-storage servers for this App and the App does not require you to create an account.

This Privacy Policy applies only to this App.

For privacy-related questions, you may contact us at: cellcave70@gmail.com

7. Data Storage and Security

Your scans, images, PDFs, recognised text, signatures and related document information are normally stored on your device.

Files stored in the App's private storage remain on your device until you:

  • Delete them;
  • Clear the App's data; or
  • Uninstall the App.

Files that you save to shared folders such as Documents, Pictures or Downloads may remain on your device after uninstalling the App.

Android's App-private storage helps protect files from access by other applications.

Information transmitted by services such as Google AdMob or Google Play is generally protected using encrypted connections.

No electronic method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We recommend using:

  • A screen lock;
  • PIN;
  • Password;
  • Fingerprint; or
  • Other supported device-security features.
