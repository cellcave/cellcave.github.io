1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Cloud Backup: Photo Storage may handle the following categories of information.

Account and Authentication Information

To create and use an account, the App may handle:

  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Authentication and verification information
  • One-time password (OTP) information
  • Sign-in events and related technical information

Your email address may be used for account registration, sign-in, account verification, security, and account-related communications.

OTP codes may be sent to your email address to verify account access and protect your account.

Where password-based authentication is supported, authentication credentials may be processed by the applicable authentication service and are not intended to be stored by Cell Cave as plain-text passwords.

Account and authentication services may use Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure.

Technical and Device Information

Depending on the features and third-party services used, the App and its service providers may process technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Device and app identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Language and general device settings
  • IP address

  • Performance information

This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, analyze, monetize, and improve the App and its services.

2. Backup Content

The App allows users to back up supported content to cloud storage.

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, supported backup content may include:

  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Documents and files
  • Audio files
  • Contacts

Backup files are encrypted on the user's device before they are transferred to cloud storage.

Encrypted backup data is stored using Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud infrastructure.

Access to Backup Contents

Backup files are encrypted before leaving the user's device.

Cell Cave does not have access to the contents of the user's encrypted backup files through the normal cloud-storage process.

The App may retrieve encrypted backup data to an authenticated user's device when the user chooses to restore their backed-up content.

Backup and restore functionality may require an internet connection.

3. Device Permissions

The App may request Android permissions when they are necessary for a feature that the user chooses to use.

Photos, Videos and Media

The App may request access to photos, videos, and other supported media so users can select content for backup or restore.

Where supported by Android, users may be able to choose:

  • Full access
  • Limited access
  • No access

The App uses the level of access granted by the user through Android's permission controls.

Contacts

The App may request Contacts permission when the user chooses to back up or restore contacts.

Contacts access is used to provide the supported contact backup and restore functionality.

Other Permissions

If additional permissions such as camera, nearby-device, file, or other device access are introduced or required for a specific feature, the App will request those permissions through the applicable Android permission mechanism and use them for the related functionality.

Users can manage or revoke applicable permissions through Android device settings.

If a required permission is denied, the related feature may not be available.

11. Data Storage and Retention

Encrypted Backup Data

Encrypted backup files may remain stored in AWS cloud infrastructure while they are associated with an active account and backup service.

Backup files are encrypted on the user's device before transfer to cloud storage.

Cell Cave does not have access to the contents of the user's encrypted backup files through the normal cloud-storage process.

Account Information

Email addresses, account identifiers, authentication information, verification information, and related account records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide account, security, backup, and related services.

Analytics, Advertising, Billing and Diagnostics

Information processed by Google, AWS, advertising partners, or other third-party providers may be retained according to the applicable provider's retention practices and policies.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, purpose of processing, legal requirements, and the provider's applicable policies.

Account Deletion and Backup Data

We keep your backed-up content and account data for as long as your account is active.

When your account is deleted, your account and associated backup data are handled according to the deletion process described on our dedicated Account & Data Deletion page.

We do not keep your files as active user-accessible backup data after account deletion.

Short-lived copies may remain in infrastructure provider backups for a limited period where technically necessary for backup, security, disaster recovery, fraud prevention, or other legitimate operational purposes.

Support Communications

If you contact Cell Cave for support, we may retain the communication for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain appropriate support records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations.
