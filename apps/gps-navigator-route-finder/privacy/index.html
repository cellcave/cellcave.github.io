Introduction

CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("Cell Cave," "we," "us," or "our") operates the GPS Navigator mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information GPS Navigator accesses, collects, processes, stores, or shares when you use the App. It also explains how certain third-party services integrated into the App may process information.

GPS Navigator provides GPS navigation, maps, route planning, nearby-place search, weather information, GPS camera features, scanning, translation, voice features, and related travel and utility tools.

By using GPS Navigator, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect and Process

The type of information processed depends on the features you choose to use and the permissions you grant.

Location Information

GPS Navigator may access your approximate and precise location when you use location-based features.

Location information may be used for:

  • Displaying your current position on a map;
  • GPS navigation and turn-by-turn directions;
  • Route calculation and re-routing;
  • Searching for destinations;
  • Finding nearby places and services;
  • Weather and location-based information;
  • Elevation and radar information;
  • Public webcam and map-related features;
  • GPS Camera functionality;
  • Saving locations, routes, or trips selected by you.

When an online navigation, mapping, weather, search, or similar service is used, coordinates or location-related requests may be transmitted to the relevant third-party service provider so that the requested result can be provided.

Location access can be disabled at any time through your Android device settings. Some features may not work correctly without location permission.

2. Camera and Image Information

GPS Navigator may request access to your device camera for features including:

  • GPS Camera;
  • QR code and barcode scanning;
  • OCR;
  • Text recognition.

GPS photographs created through the App may contain location information if you use GPS Camera functionality.

Photos and related GPS Camera records created by the App are primarily stored locally on your device.

GPS Navigator does not request broad permission to read your entire photo or video library in the inspected application version.

Camera access occurs only when you use a feature that requires it and after Android permission is granted.

3. Microphone and Voice Information

GPS Navigator may request microphone access when you use voice-enabled features, including:

  • Voice search;
  • Voice translation;
  • Speech input.

The App uses Android speech-recognition functionality. Depending on your Android device, settings, language, and speech-recognition provider, voice input may be processed locally on your device or transmitted to the applicable speech-recognition service.

GPS Navigator does not require continuous microphone access for normal navigation.

We do not intentionally store raw voice recordings on our own servers.

4. Search, Navigation and Route Information

When you use online navigation or search functionality, GPS Navigator may process information such as:

  • Destination names;
  • Search terms;
  • Start location;
  • Destination coordinates;
  • Current coordinates;
  • Route points;
  • Intermediate waypoints;
  • Nearby search locations;
  • Route preferences.

This information may be transmitted to mapping, routing, geocoding, or location-service providers to return the requested navigation or search result.

5. Locally Stored Information

Certain information may be stored locally on your device to provide App functionality.

This may include:

  • Saved places;
  • Favorite locations;
  • Saved trips;
  • Navigation preferences;
  • GPS photographs;
  • Scan history;
  • QR or barcode history;
  • Translation history;
  • Favorite webcams;
  • App preferences;
  • Privacy or advertising consent selections;
  • Other feature settings.

Local information may generally be deleted by removing the relevant item inside the App, clearing GPS Navigator's application data through Android settings, or uninstalling the App.

6. Device, Usage and Advertising Information

GPS Navigator integrates advertising, analytics, messaging, and measurement technologies.

Depending on your device, privacy choices, consent status, and feature usage, these services may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers;
  • Advertising identifiers;
  • Firebase installation identifiers;
  • App version;
  • Android version;
  • Device type;
  • IP address;
  • General network information;
  • App interactions;
  • Feature usage;
  • Advertisement interactions;
  • Installation and attribution information;
  • Consent information;
  • Diagnostic information where applicable.

This information may be used to operate the App, measure performance, show and measure advertising, detect fraud or abuse, understand feature usage, deliver notifications, and improve App functionality.

Information processed through GPS Navigator may be used to:
