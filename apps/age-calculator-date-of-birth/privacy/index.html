CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("Cell Cave," "we," "us," or "our") provides the Age Calculator: Date of Birth mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App and describes your privacy choices.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

1. App Functionality

The App lets you enter dates, including a date of birth, to calculate age, date differences, and related results.

Dates entered for calculations are processed locally on your device.

Cell Cave does not require you to create an account, and we do not receive or store your entered date of birth on our servers as part of the App's normal operation.

2. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

The App does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or precise location.

It does not offer account registration and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, videos, audio files, or precise device location for its core age-calculation features.

3. Information Processed by Third-Party Services

Although Cell Cave does not directly collect the dates you enter, third-party services integrated into the App may automatically process limited technical and usage information.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and settings, this may include:

  • Device and app identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted
  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, and general device settings
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address
  • App interactions, session information, feature usage, and ad interactions
  • Diagnostic, performance, and similar technical information
  • Consent status and advertising preferences

This information does not include the date of birth or other dates you enter into the calculator.

4. Advertising and Consent Management

The App may display advertisements provided through Google AdMob.

AdMob and its advertising partners may use device identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit, and protect advertisements.

The App uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), also referred to as the Google consent form , to request, record, and communicate applicable privacy and advertising choices.

UMP may process information such as your consent selections, device or app identifiers, IP-based region information, and technical information needed to determine which consent message should be displayed.

Where required by law, the App requests your consent before using information for personalized advertising.

If you do not consent, you may still receive non-personalized or limited ads.

You can manage applicable advertising choices through the consent form shown in the App, your device settings, and Google's advertising controls.

Google may receive limited technical and diagnostic information associated with the service.

Cell Cave does not use ML Kit to send your entered date of birth to its own servers.
