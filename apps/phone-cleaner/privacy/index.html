This Privacy Policy explains how CELL CAVE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("CELL CAVE," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Phone Cleaner Android application (package name: com.clearner.mobilecleaner.filemanager.cloud.savevideo.file.photo) (the "App").

CELL CAVE is the developer of the App and, where data-protection law uses these terms, the "controller" or "business" responsible for the personal information described in this Policy, except for information that Google or other providers process independently under their own terms.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy. Where the law requires your consent, for example for personalized advertising in some regions, we ask for it separately and you may withdraw it at any time.

1. Summary

  • Your content stays on your device. Cleaning, file management, duplicate-photo detection, image compression, App Manager, notification cleaning, clipboard cleaning, the Recycle Bin, the Private Vault and the in-app assistant work locally. We do not upload your files, photos, videos, notifications, clipboard text, installed-app list, app usage history or Vault content to our servers, and we do not operate a server for user content.
  • No accounts. The App has no registration, login or user accounts.
  • Third-party services process some data. Google AdMob may process advertising-related information. Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config process analytics, diagnostics and configuration information. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) is used to deliver push notifications and messages. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) / Google Consent Form is used to obtain and manage applicable privacy and advertising consent where required. Google ML Kit is used to provide machine-learning-based features within the App. Google Play processes subscription and purchase information, as described in Sections 9 to 12.
  • Data is protected in transit. Data transmitted by the App to the third-party services described in this Policy is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS where supported and implemented by those providers.
  • You have choices. You can revoke permissions, change advertising choices, delete your Android Advertising ID, clear App data, uninstall the App and contact us to exercise your privacy rights.

2. Features of the App

The App provides:

  • Quick Clean and Deep Clean of junk and cache files
  • Large Files finder
  • Duplicate, similar and screenshot photo detection
  • Image compression, where compressed copies are saved and originals are kept
  • File manager
  • App Manager for app sizes, last-used dates, unused apps and uninstall shortcuts
  • On-device permission audit
  • Notification Cleaner
  • Clipboard Cleaner
  • Phone Boost, CPU and battery information screens
  • Recycle Bin
  • PIN- and biometric-protected Private Vault
  • On-device "AI" analyzer and assistant that suggests cleaning actions
  • Scheduled automatic cleaning
  • Advertising
  • Push notifications
  • Optional Premium subscriptions that remove ads

The App's analyzer and assistant features use rules and calculations performed on your device. They do not send your files, photos, questions or device information to an artificial-intelligence service.

3. Information Processed Only on Your Device

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App accesses and processes the following information locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to CELL CAVE or to the third-party services named in this Policy:

  • Photos, videos, audio, documents and other files, and their names, paths, sizes, types and dates
  • Duplicate-photo, similar-photo, screenshot and large-file analysis results
  • Compressed image copies you create
  • The list of installed apps, their package names, sizes, versions and requested permissions
  • App usage statistics, such as when an app was last used, if you grant Usage Access
  • Active notifications, including sending app, title, text and notification key, if you grant Notification Access
  • Clipboard content, when you use the Clipboard Cleaner
  • Device storage, memory (RAM), CPU, battery and operating-system information
  • App settings, language, theme, cleaning history, automatic-cleaning schedules and Recycle Bin records
  • Private Vault files, the Vault index, a salted hash of your Vault PIN and related Vault settings

Because this information is processed only on your device and is not transmitted off the device, CELL CAVE does not receive it.

5. Files, Media, the Recycle Bin and Image Compression

File scanning, duplicate detection, compression and deletion happen on your device.

When supported, files removed through cleaning or file-management features are moved to the App's local Recycle Bin, where you can restore them for 7 days.

After 7 days, Recycle Bin items are permanently deleted.

Files that Android does not allow the App to move may be permanently deleted immediately after you confirm.

Compressed images are saved as new copies; originals are kept unless you delete them.

6. Installed Apps, Usage Access and the Permission Audit

The App reads your installed-app list and each app's requested permissions to show app sizes, provide uninstall shortcuts and run an on-device permission audit.

The permission audit is a heuristic that highlights apps requesting sensitive permissions; it is not a malware scanner.

If you grant Usage Access, the App reads local usage statistics to show when apps were last opened.

This information is processed only on your device and is never sold, shared, or used for analytics or advertising.

7. Notification Access and Clipboard Cleaner

If you enable Notification Access, the App reads active notifications, including sending app, title, text and notification key, so it can display and dismiss clearable notifications at your request.

Notification content is processed in device memory, is not stored as a notification history and is not transmitted off your device.

You can revoke Notification Access in Android Settings.

When you use the Clipboard Cleaner, the App checks and clears the Android clipboard on your device.

Clipboard content is not stored or transmitted by the App.

8. Private Vault and Biometric Authentication

The Private Vault copies files you select into app-private storage on your device and, with your confirmation through Android's system dialog, removes the originals.

Vault files are not uploaded to CELL CAVE or to any cloud service and are not included in the App's backup rules.

Your Vault PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as readable text.

If you use biometric unlocking, Android performs the verification and the App receives only a success or failure result; the App never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data or biometric template.

Important: Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App permanently deletes Vault files stored on your device.

We cannot recover Vault files or reset a forgotten PIN because we have no copy of them. Keep separate backups of irreplaceable files.

13. Notifications and Automatic Cleaning

If you turn on automatic cleaning, the App uses Android's background-work system to scan or remove eligible temporary files accessible to it, subject to Android platform restrictions, and to permanently delete expired Recycle Bin items after seven days.

The App may generate local notifications about automatic-cleaning results or storage reminders.

These local notifications are generated on your device and are not used for advertising.

Separately, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) as described in Section 11.4 to deliver push notifications and messages.

You can turn off automatic cleaning in the App and disable App notifications in Android Settings.

14. Android Backup and Device Transfer

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up the App's preference files to your Google Account, and may copy them during a device-to-device transfer.

These preferences may include privacy-acceptance status and date, language, theme, automatic-cleaning settings and Vault settings, which can include the salted hash of your Vault PIN.

Premium entitlement data is excluded from cloud backup but may be copied during a direct device transfer.

Vault files, Recycle Bin files and other files are not included in the App's backup rules.

Backups are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy , and you can manage them in your Android or Google Account settings.
