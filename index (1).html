1. Overview

Age Calculator: Date of Birth is provided by CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED.

The app allows users to enter date information and calculate age and related date results.

The app does not require a CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED account, login, name, email address, phone number, or password to use its main functionality.

2. Information We Handle

Information we do not directly request

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • A CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED account or password
  • Precise GPS location
  • Credit or debit card details

Technical and usage information

The app uses third-party analytics and advertising technologies. Depending on the provider, device settings, region, and applicable privacy choices, these services may process technical information such as:

  • Device and app information
  • Operating system information
  • App interactions and usage events
  • Advertising or app identifiers
  • IP address and network information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic or performance information
  • Approximate location inferred from technical information such as an IP address
Precise Location

Age Calculator: Date of Birth does not request precise GPS location for its core functionality.

Contacting support

If you voluntarily contact CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED by email, we receive the email address you use, your message, and any information you choose to include so we can respond to your request.

3. Date of Birth and Calculation Data

The date of birth or other date information you enter into the app is used to perform the requested calculations.

This calculation data is handled locally on your device as part of the app's normal functionality.

CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED does not upload the date of birth you enter to a CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED server as part of the app's normal age calculation functionality.

4. Device Permissions

Age Calculator: Date of Birth does not require access to your photos, videos, files, contacts, microphone, camera, precise location, or device storage for its core functionality.

The app's age and date calculations can be used without granting these types of device permissions.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Date of birth and date calculation information entered for the app's normal calculations is handled locally on the device and is not stored on CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED servers as part of the normal calculation process.

Analytics and advertising information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to each provider's own retention practices.

If you contact CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED for support, we may retain your support communication for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or maintain appropriate support records.

9. Your Privacy Choices

  • You can manage advertising and privacy options available through your device settings.
  • You can use applicable consent controls presented by third-party advertising services.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.
  • You can contact CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED regarding applicable privacy questions or requests.

Privacy rights and available choices may vary depending on your country or region.

10. Children's Privacy

Age Calculator: Date of Birth is not directed to children under the age of 13.

CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED does not knowingly require children under 13 to create an account or directly provide personal contact information through the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has directly provided personal information to CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED, please contact us.

11. Data Security

Date calculation information used through the app's normal functionality is handled locally on the user's device rather than being uploaded to CELL CAVE ( SMC-PRIVATE ) LIMITED servers.

Information processed by Firebase or advertising providers is subject to the security practices of those third-party providers.

No electronic system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International Data Processing

Third-party analytics and advertising providers may operate in multiple countries.

Technical, analytics, or advertising information may therefore be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Such processing is subject to each provider's privacy practices and applicable requirements.
